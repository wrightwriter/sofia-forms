Intro
A single-day hangout, workshop and jam session where multidisciplinary artists gather to showcase their art, workflows and share knowledge.
All forms of art are welcome — the medium matters less than the person behind it and what they're trying to express. Whether you are a musician looking to visualize your music, someone curious about modular synths who never got hands-on, or a shader artist looking for a soundtrack — this is the place.
What you can expect
A wide variety of short talks, open discussions and people to meet.
The day winds down into an open HDMI/audio cable jam session at the afterparty — bring a laptop, a synth, a camera, whatever you've got. Plug in and play.
Want to give a talk or join the jam?
Our format is short talks during the day, then the open jam session in the evening. We're open to new participants for both — you can grab us on the day of the event, but ideally drop us a line at sofia.forms.sf@gmail.com beforehand. Halfbaked ideas welcome, it doesn't have to be serious.
Some confirmed talks
  • workflows for live audio performance
  • different methods for audio visualization
  • procedural music
  • procedural visual art
  • building your own analog and digital instruments
