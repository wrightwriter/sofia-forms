MAY 16 2026
A single-day hangout, workshop and party where multidisciplinary artists gather to showcase their art, workflows and share knowledge. Warmup for Sofia Synth Symposium 2026 on June 12-14!
All forms of art are welcome — the medium matters less than the person behind it and what they're trying to express. Whether you are a musician looking to visualize your music, someone curious about modular synths who never got hands-on, or a shader artist looking for a soundtrack — this is the place.
